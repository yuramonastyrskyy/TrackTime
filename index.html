TrackTime

Put the phone on a tripod at the edge of the track, lined up with the finish line, so the white line runs down the middle of the picture.

  1. Drag the two markers onto the finish line: one where it meets the top of the track, one at the inner edge.
  2. Connect the Bluetooth speaker.
  3. Tap Start timing while the line is clear.
